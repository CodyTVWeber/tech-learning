# Payments

Use this skill when recording, comparing, or closing a payment obligation.

## Amount kinds

Keep these three figures distinct. Never overwrite one with another.

| Kind | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Estimated | A forecast before anyone agrees to pay |
| Agreed | The amount the parties accepted |
| Receipted | The amount actually paid, as shown on a receipt or statement |

If the figures differ, record the variance and why. Do not "reconcile" them by quietly replacing an agreed amount with an estimate or a receipt.

## Closing an obligation

Close an obligation only when the recipient and the amount both match the record.

## Research is not authorization

Finding a likely payee, an invoice, or a recommended amount is research. It does not authorize a payment, a transfer, or a change to an agreed figure. Authorization is a separate, explicit step.
