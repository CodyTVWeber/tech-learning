# Credits — Drum Line Shed

- The eight snare lines are original patterns written for this app. They are not taken from published etudes, rudimental solos, or songs.
- Snare and click sounds are synthesized in the browser with Web Audio. No audio files are shipped.
- The staff, noteheads, rests, and drum pad are drawn in SVG and CSS for this page.
- `images/shed-chart.png` and `images/shell-stamp.png` are solid placeholder fields from `tools/homeschool/png.py`, kept for a later picture pass. The play screen does not pretend they are photographs.
- Scripture on the set list is Psalm 100:1, King James Version (public domain): “Make a joyful noise unto the LORD, all ye lands.”
