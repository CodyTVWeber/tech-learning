# History credits

## Photographs

Documentary-style pictures generated for this app (station, tickets, stamp album, wheels, lantern, sailing ship, civic hall). No learner photos. No logos.

## Text

- Original station notes, quizzes, timeline labels, and songs (words and WebAudio tunes).
- Preamble to the U.S. Constitution and Bill of Rights: public-domain United States government text.
- Office-holder list (presidents 1–47) is a public fact list, original question wording.

## Not included

Classical Conversations recitation sentences, timeline-card wording, songs, or audio. Topic *titles* only.

## Sound

Web Speech API when the browser has a voice; WebAudio whistle, chuff, punch, and bell. No audio files.
