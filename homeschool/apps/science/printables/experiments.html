Leaf under a lens

Date ________Place ________Grown-up nearby

Lab 2 · Field Expedition

Celery color climb

Date ________Adult pours dye

Gather: pale celery, water, food dye, jar. Dye stains — smock on.

Do not drink the dye water. Ask a grown-up to pour.
  1. Grown-up: water + a few drops of dye in a jar.
  2. Stand a cut celery stick in the jar.
  3. Wait hours, then overnight.
  4. Split the stalk. Look for colored tubes.
  5. Those tubes are how a plant drinks. Log colors below.

Lab 4 · Field Expedition

Water cycle in a bag

Date ________Window facing ________

  1. Spoon a little water into a bag and seal it.
  2. Tape it to a sunny window.

Lab 5 · Field Expedition

Fizz jar — acid meets base

Date ________Adult required

Gather: baking soda, vinegar, jar, tray.

Do this with a grown-up. Do not taste. No sealed bottles — gas needs a way out.
  1. Jar on a tray. Spoon of baking soda (a base).
  2. Pour a little vinegar (an acid).
  3. Watch the fizz. The gas is carbon dioxide.
  4. Rinse. Log: acid + base can make a salt, water, and a gas.

Lab 6 · Field Expedition

Pulse after a walk

Date ________
Walk, do not sprint to dizziness. This is curiosity, not a medical test. No names, no health notes beyond counts.
  1. Sit still. Find a pulse with two fingers, not a thumb.
  2. Count beats for 15 seconds. Multiply by 4. Rest count: ______
  3. Walk two minutes. Sit. Count again. Walk count: ______
  4. The heart is a pump that matches the work.

Lab 8 · Field Expedition

Shadow stick

Do not stare at the sun. Hats on hot days. Grown-up if you go outside.
  1. Stand a stick in soil. Mark the shadow tip with a pebble.
  2. Wait. Mark again.
  3. The sun’s path writes the shadow’s swing.
  4. Optional reading while you wait: Psalm 19:1 (KJV).

Lab 10 · Field Expedition

Spool pulley

No necks in string. Adult ties the high end.
  1. Grown-up hangs a spool so it turns.
  2. Run string over the groove. Tie a cup to one end.
  3. Pull the other end. The cup rises as you pull down.
  4. You changed the direction of work.
