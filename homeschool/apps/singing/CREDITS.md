## Songs
Public-domain hymn and children’s texts only:

- Jesus Loves Me — Anna Warner (1860), William Bradbury (1862)
- Amazing Grace — John Newton (1779), NEW BRITAIN
- The Doxology — Thomas Ken (1674), OLD HUNDREDTH
- Holy, Holy, Holy — Reginald Heber (1826), John B. Dykes (1861)
- What a Friend We Have in Jesus — Joseph Scriven (1855), Charles Converse (1868)
- This Is My Father’s World — Maltbie Babcock (1901), Franklin Sheppard (1915); public domain in the US
- Come, Thou Fount — Robert Robinson (1758), NETTLETON
- Fairest Lord Jesus — Münster Gesangbuch (1677), CRUSADER’S HYMN
- The B-I-B-L-E — traditional children’s gospel
- I’ve Got the Joy — traditional gospel chorus
- Jesus Loves the Little Children — C. H. Woolston / George F. Root
- Hot Cross Buns, Are You Sleeping (Frère Jacques) — traditional, for baby pitch/rhythm

Original learning songs written for this app: High Bird Low Bird, The Steady Nest, Climb the Branch, Echo in the Pines, Soft as a Feather, Morning Choir Call.

No Classical Conversations songs. No copyrighted movie solfege lyrics.

## Sound
All pitches, hymn tunes, clicks, and chirps are synthesized in Web Audio. Read-aloud uses `speechSynthesis` when present. Microphone audio, if allowed, is analysed in-page and is not stored or uploaded.

Pitch detector is a standard time-domain autocorrelation method implemented here (not copied from a copyrighted lesson plan).
