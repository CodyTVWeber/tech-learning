# Treasure Voyage — credits

- App copy, techniques, and practice items are original. They are aligned to public math skills (counting, facts, place value, fractions, decimals, money, time, measurement, geometry, long operations, word problems). They are **not** a copy of Classical Conversations Math Map pages, notation cards, or recitation text.
- Skip-count topic titles (2s, 3s, 5s, … squares, cubes) and public measurement equivalents (3 tsp = 1 tbsp, 12 in = 1 ft, metric prefixes, area formulas) are public-domain facts with original wording.
- On-screen art is original SVG/CSS (brass coins with a ship stamp, island markers, hull, chests, crew portholes). PNG slots in `images/` remain for the later picture wave and are not used as counting tokens.
- Sounds are synthesized at runtime with the Web Audio API. No audio files and no third-party samples.
- Type: Palatino / Georgia for the chart; Trebuchet MS / Gill Sans for buttons (system fonts only).
- Untested, awaiting parent test.
